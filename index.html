      About this site

      Aotearoa Unpacked gathers official statistics from Stats NZ, government ministries and established industry bodies, and presents them as simple charts with short, neutral explanations. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of the agencies whose data it uses.

      Every chart names its source and links to the original release. Many figures, especially migration estimates, are provisional and are revised in later releases, so check the source before relying on a number for anything important.
